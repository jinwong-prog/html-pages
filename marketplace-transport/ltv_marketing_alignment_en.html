01 — What Predicted LTV isPredicted LTV 是什麼

What is Predicted Future LTV?Predicted Future LTV 是什麼？

Predicted Future LTV = how much Platform Take we expect this customer to generate in the future.Predicted Future LTV = 預計這個客戶未來可為平台帶來多少收入。

It combines how many completed-order days we expect × Platform Take on those days.由「預計有多少個完成單日 × 有完成單日的平台收入」計算。

Not GTV. Not money already earned.不是 GTV，也不是已經賺到的錢。

03 — Understanding p_alive / Model理解 p_alive／模型

Understanding p_alive: from past behaviour to the cut-off date理解 p_alive：從過往行為，看截止日的狀態

p_alive tells us whether a customer is likely still active at the cut-off date. It is different from predicting whether they will purchase again in the future.p_alive 告訴我們客戶在截止日是否仍可能活躍；它與預測客戶未來會否再次購買是兩件事。

1 · What is p_alive?

Is the customer still active at the cut-off date?客戶在截止日仍然活躍嗎？

p_alive uses the customer’s past purchase behaviour to estimate the likelihood that they are still active at the cut-off date.p_alive 利用客戶過往的購買行為，估算他們在截止日仍然活躍的機率。

Data interpretation如何讀欄

  • Historical historical_completed_order_days / historical_completed_orders = facts up to the cutoff歷史完成訂單日／張是截止日前事實
  • Predicted days / orders = 180D forecast. Cell unit d = completed-order days, not calendar days預測日／張是未來 180 日預測。d＝完成訂單日，不是日曆日
  • 180D No Completed Order Observed = Yes if actual_day_platform_take_180d = 0 after cutoff. No = a completed order was seen; then read Bias. Holdout fact. Not Predicted Return ProbabilityYes＝截止日後 180 日實際平台收入為 0。No＝有完成單，才看 Bias。是回測事實，不是回流機率
  • To Next T / To Next P = historical dollar gaps, not an upgrade probability升到下一 T／P 還差多少錢是已賺金額缺口，不是升級機率
  • Prediction Bias = (Predicted − Actual) ÷ Actual on 180D Platform Take; 0 = on target. Predicted = Actual (including both $0) → 0%. Predicted not $0 and Actual = $0 → blank. Headline accuracy uses company total預測金額偏差百分比；（Predicted − Actual）÷ Actual。兩邊都是 0 顯示 0%。Predicted 不是 0 且 Actual 是 0 才空白。公司準確度用全體加總

Three Different Churn Measures三種不同的流失指標

Two numbers are for one customer. Churn Rate is for a group. The time direction is the real difference — already stopped as of the cut-off, may leave in the next N days, or already left in a past reporting period.兩個數字是「這一位客戶」。流失率是「一群客戶」。真正的分別是時間方向：截至截止日可能已經走了、未來 N 日會否走、還是回頭看一段期間實際走了多少。

What makes p_alive go up or down?甚麼會令 p_alive 上升或下降？

It comes down to one simple question: Has this customer been quiet for longer than we would normally expect from them?簡單來說，就是看：這位客人是否比平常「沉默」得更久？

1. How long since their last order — compared with their usual buying pattern.1. 距離上次落單有多久——但要跟這位客人自己平常的購買習慣比較。
If they normally order regularly but have now been quiet for much longer than usual, their p_alive will be lower.如果他平時經常落單，但現在比平常沉默得更久，p_alive 就會較低。

2. How frequently they ordered in the past.2. 他過往落單有多頻密。
The same period of inactivity can mean very different things for different customers.同樣一段時間沒有落單，對不同客人可以代表完全不同的意思。

For example:例如：

  • Usually orders every week, but has been quiet for 2 months → strong warning sign → lower p_alive平時每星期落單，但突然兩個月沒有落單 → 明顯警號 → p_alive 較低
  • Usually orders only every few months, and has been quiet for 2 months → not unusual → p_alive may still be high平時本來就是幾個月才落一次單，兩個月沒有落單 → 不算異常 → p_alive 仍可能較高

Same silence, different verdict — because every customer has a different buying pattern.同樣沉默兩個月，判斷可以不同——因為每位客人的購買習慣都不一樣。

In short: the model compares each customer with their own ordering history, rather than simply counting the days since their last order.簡單來說：模型是將客人跟「自己的過往習慣」比較，而不是單純看距離上次落單有多少天。

Special case: if a customer has only ordered once, there is not enough repeat-order history to tell whether their behaviour has changed. In this model output, p_alive therefore stays at 100%.特殊情況：如果客人只落過一次單，模型沒有足夠的重複購買紀錄去判斷他的行為是否已經改變，因此在這個模型輸出中，p_alive 會維持在 100%。
