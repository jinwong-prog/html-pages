Internal QA sample — not a CRM list. Status: To Validate.

The model finds the right customers, but it predicts too little money.

At 180 days the model predicted HK$346,312 of Platform Take. Customers actually gave HK$456,809. That is a calibration ratio of 0.758 — 24.2% below actual. Order counts are much closer: 0.876 (21,169 predicted vs 24,160 actual completed orders). Two things are low at once: active order-days (0.900) and money per active order-day (0.842). Multiply them and you get the money ratio. Section ③ shows this split.

This page is a check on numbers only. It is not a decision to release the model, and it does not say who to call. (queried 2026-08-12, HK TRANSPORT PlatformOD van; customer key user_unique_id, as_of_date 2024-12-31, holdout 2026-06-30)

⑨ How the numbers were made

Method notes

Two standard models are fitted once per ranking date, then cut to every horizon:

  • BG/NBD predicts how many active order-days a customer still has (predicted_active_order_days_180d), from frequency, recency and T.
  • Gamma-Gamma predicts money per active order-day (predicted_day_platform_take_per_active_order_day).
  • Multiply the two to get predicted_day_platform_take_180d.

Platform Take is the one money definition in this project:

platform_take → day_platform_take (foundation.hk_transport_platformod_van_user_daily_transactions_since_2018)
historical_platform_take = SUM(day_platform_take) to as_of
  • driver_commission is not platform revenue and is not used here.
  • Official Tiers are ranked on history only (historical_platform_take), 11 groups from P100 down to P10. A prediction never moves a customer's tier.
  • 3,437/10,010 customers have monetary_fallback = TRUE: they had too little repeat history for Gamma-Gamma, so a tier median was used (monetary_fallback_method).
  • Both models were fitted on this 10,000-customer sample. The fitted parameters are therefore not the full-population parameters, and neither are the ratios on this page.
  • The sample takes 910 customers per Official Tier, so every tier carries the same customer weight here. The real base does not look like that. Read the tier rows, not the "All" row, if you want per-group accuracy.
  • Money per active order-day is learned from monetary_value_platform, the average day_platform_take per historical active order-day since 2018. If the market moves after 2024-12-31, the model cannot see it. Section ③ shows how much this costs.
  • Prediction error = predicted minus actual. A negative number means the model was low.
