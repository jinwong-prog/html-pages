What BG/NBD looks at · then we check it on P99

BG/NBD uses three history numbers to guess will they order again (not how much money per order — that is Gamma-Gamma):

  • frequency — how many times they ordered before (repeat rhythm)
  • recency — first order → last order (days). Not “days since last order”.
  • T — first order → ranking day (how long we watched them)

Stay signal = recency ÷ T. Near 1 = last order near the ranking day. Near 0 = last order early, then long silence. Days since last order ≈ T − recency (a different number — do not call that recency).

Next section is not the model’s stay score table. We take homepage P99 (high-value tier), split by recency ÷ T high vs low, then count who really ordered again in the next 91 days — a real-order check.

In short: high value ≠ same chance of coming back.

So what

  • High-value and ordered recently → keep serving · avoid needless discounts
  • High-value but silent a long time → do not treat as the same return risk
  • P99 on the homepage: Service first · Avoid discounts
