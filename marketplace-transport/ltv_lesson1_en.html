How we chose “18% annual decline” · P99 example
Simple question · P99 scope · Month 1

Simple question: after a customer places their first order, how much does GoGoX earn from them each year — and how fast does that shrink? Numbers below = P99 only (next 9% · first order in 2024 Jan–Jun · still in Home P99 on 2026-03-31). Month 1 = the month of the first order · then month 2, 3, and so on.

Table 2 · How we estimate yearly decline

How Table 2 works · After Month 6

After Month 6, we compare customer value over two periods that are 12 months apart. For example, we compare the average value from Months 7–9 with Months 19–21. The difference gives us the estimated annual decline. Missing months are counted as zero. The four 3-month checks show a decline of roughly 16–20%. To make the estimate more stable, we then use a longer 6-month period, which gives a result around the middle of that range.

Summary · Decline % · Honeymoon curve

Summary:
Table 1 shows which periods we leave out of the calculation — the early months and the full-year comparison window.
Table 2 shows each check of how much customer value decreases after Month 6. Each comparison gives us one annual decline rate.
For P99, we compare the average value in Months 7–12 with the average value in Months 19–24. This indicates an annual decline of about 18% — 18.1% in BQ.
In the Home table, each customer group uses its own annual decline rate calculated using the same method (mart_ltv_tier_annual_decay).
The company-wide 15% rate is only used as a default when a group has too little data or the results are too noisy.
Therefore, P99 uses 18.1%, not the 15% default rate.

Decline %:
Based on the checks completed on 2026-08-10 （queried 2026-08-10，HK TRANSPORT PlatformOD · P99，2024H1 first-order ∩ Home P99 rank 2026-03-31，monthly platform_take）：

  • 3-month checks: 20.3% → 18.0% → 17.1% → 15.6% decline
  • 6-month comparison: 18.1% annual decline
  • Full-year comparison: 23.6% decline
  • Sample size: approximately 63,800 customers

Honeymoon curve recheck:
We also rechecked the pattern where customer value is higher during the first few months （queried 2026-08-10，same scope，n≈63,784）：

  • Month 1 average value: ~HK$62.98 / person / month (avg monthly platform_take)
  • Month 1 is about 1.61× higher than the average value in Months 7–12
  • Months 1–6 are roughly 1.10–1.21× higher than the later-period average
  • Customer value starts to stabilize around Month 7
