What it means (in one glance)

The signs are NOT random — they form one chain. When a driver grabs the customer's re-order (the loop), 93% of them also had a job die with no other driver taking it, and 84% had the customer never come back on their own.
So the loop is not a one-off — it sits on top of the full harm chain: give back → let it die → grab the re-order.
Bot-speed drivers also over-drop. Drivers flagged too-fast (bot) are 6× more likely to also drop far more orders than their peers (39% of them, vs 6% across the whole pool) — same behaviour, machine speed.
