Spotting cheat-app (bot) drivers — candidate list v1

HK truck orders (van / mudou / mudou9), public-pool drivers only. Company-assigned (OA) drivers skipped.
This is a list for people to check by hand — not a verdict, and no penalty. Read the caveat at the end.
(queried 2026-07-22, HK TRANSPORT, 30-day entry / 90-day signals)

Among 3,101 public-pool truck drivers who really do business, we flagged 1,451 drivers for a person to review by hand (top tier: high_priority_review).
These drivers show the pattern we care about: they take a job, give it back, no other driver takes it, and often the same driver then grabs the customer's re-order and finishes it.

"Really do business" = in the last 30 days, given ≥30 orders and finished ≥30 orders (orders_assigned_30d >= 30, orders_completed_30d >= 30).

What the warning signs are, and how many drivers show each

Each sign adds points to a driver's warning score (0–10). Counted among the 3,101 drivers who do enough business. Rates shown as n/N (%).

Real columns behind these: took-then-gave-back = event_type_cd = 4, actor_type = 'Driver'; no other driver took it = order ended status = 'cancelled' with a event_type_cd = 8 (cannot_find_driver) or 11 (expired) event; same-driver loop = same_driver_took_the_reorder_90d; the burst sign = at_least_8_release_or_drop_events_in_60_seconds. Warning score = signal_points_total.

Note on the burst sign. The table today counts "8+ take-backs or grabs-and-lets-go inside 60 seconds" (at_least_8_release_or_drop_events_in_60_seconds). A tighter, cleaner bot-speed sign — 3+ different orders grabbed and let go inside 8 seconds — is planned but not built yet, so it is not used in these numbers.

What this does NOT say (read before acting)

  • This is a review list, not a verdict. It goes to a person (Ops + Trust & Safety) to check by hand. We do not recommend any penalty here.
  • No dollar figure yet. The harm in money (net_impact_hkd) needs the LTV model, which is not built. Until then, no punishment.
  • Distance is not in yet. The driving-distance number (per-km value, from OSMnx) is still being loaded. "Keeps the pricey trips" is judged on trip value (GTV) for now, not value per km.
  • No distance and no amount shown in this v1 on purpose — those come in a later version.
